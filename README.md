### ALGO-000007: Strong Exponential Time Hypothesis (SETH)
- **Subfield:** fine-grained complexity
- **Open:** Is solving k-SAT with n variables and unbounded k essentially 2^n time, i.e., does the SETH constant s_inf = lim s_k equal 1?
- **Status:** Open since 1999 (Impagliazzo–Paturi). Known: sparsification lemma reduces dense-to-sparse instances; SETH implies lower bounds for dozens of problems (drag reductions of n^(2-ε) for Edit Distance, etc.).
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** The load-bearing assumption of fine-grained complexity; its failure would collapse a web of conditional lower bounds.
- **Key works:** R. Impagliazzo, R. Paturi, "On the complexity of k-SAT", JCSS 2001; ETH chapter in "Fine-Grained Complexity" course notes by V. Williams (verify)
- **Aliases:** SETH; strong ETH
